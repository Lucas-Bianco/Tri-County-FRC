About Us

Who We Are

We are a FIRST Robotics Competition team of vocational students at Tri-County Regional Vocational Technical School Franklin, MA.

Since 2010

Our Story

Tri-County students first competed in the FIRST Robotics Competition in 2010 and last competed in 2019. For 2027, the program is back as with a brand new team ready to tackle any challenge.

Due to covid-19 and funding constraints, the program was suspended for a few years.

Thanks to our new Engineering Teacher, after doing Vex for 4 years, we are now excited to be back in action with a new reinstated FRC team that is ready to compete.

Our School & Shops

School Photo

Tri-County Regional Vocational Technical School is a public vocational school in Franklin, Massachusetts. Students from 11 surrounding towns attend the school to learn trades and technical skills in a variety of programs.

Students select a shop where they will learn specialized trade skills and use them while they work on the robot while building even more advanced skills.
