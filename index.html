What is FRC?

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) gives high school students a few weeks to design and build a robot of up to 115 pounds for a brand new game every year. Then competing with other teams around the world!

This is a student driven competition. Students run everything: design, manufactoring, electrical, controls, programming, and marketing.
