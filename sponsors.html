Help vocational students design, build, and program a competition robot, and help us take it as far as it can go.

Why

Why Support Us?

With your help, we will design and build a robust robot made for this year's game, compete at our first events, and work our way to higher-level competitions.

Every sponsor is invited to visit us during build season and to watch us compete in March.
